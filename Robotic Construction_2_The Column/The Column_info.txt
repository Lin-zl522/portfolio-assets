该项目是对“Closed Loop”的延续，旨在进一步探索再生塑料在熔融沉积成型（FDM）增材制造中的应用。通过将立柱作为结构载体，研究团队考察了利用增材制造元件替代传统建筑构件的可行性。项目不仅关注材料回收，也通过创新设计策略来突破规模与力学性能限制，从而拓展增材制造的应用边界。
本研究的核心挑战在于测试并建立一套完整的3D 打印部件回收流程，包括材料收集、加工、再挤出以及对打印工艺的优化。同时，项目还将评估再生塑料在流变特性、机械强度和耐久度等方面的物理性能，以确定其在建筑领域的可行性。与传统制造方法相比，增材制造常常受制于打印尺寸和结构完整性。为应对这一难题，项目采用非常规的设计手段，通过几何优化、材料分配策略及结构加固技术，提升打印部件在功能性与力学性能方面的表现。

[en]
This project continues the research developed in Closed Loop, further investigating recycled plastic in fused deposition modeling (FDM). Using a column as a structural prototype, the team examines whether additively manufactured elements can replace conventional building components. Beyond material recycling, the project uses innovative design strategies to overcome limitations of scale and mechanical performance, expanding the possible applications of additive manufacturing.

The central challenge is to test and establish a complete recycling workflow for 3D-printed components, including material collection, processing, re-extrusion, and optimization of the printing process. The project also evaluates the rheological behavior, mechanical strength, and durability of recycled plastics to determine their feasibility in architecture. Because additive manufacturing is often constrained by print size and structural integrity, the project employs unconventional methods—including geometric optimization, material distribution strategies, and structural reinforcement—to improve the functional and mechanical performance of printed components.
