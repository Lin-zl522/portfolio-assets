Tiburtina，集合火车站与巴士站于一体，长期以来都是许多移民进入欧洲的门户。过去，这里是立交桥纵横交错的空间，日常居民活动与公共交通设施在此交织。由于罗马对移民的漠视政策，许多移民只能在这些立交桥夹缝中艰难谋生。
近期，Tiburtina 周边的整修使得这一区域的空间形态变得难以利用。针对这一状况以及不友好的移民政策，本项目提出在此打造一个带有纪念碑的新公共空间，为本地居民和移民提供新的生活区域和临时庇护所。

[en]
Tiburtina, which brings together a railway station and a bus terminal, has long served as a gateway for many migrants entering Europe. Historically, the area was defined by a network of intersecting overpasses, where the daily activities of local residents intertwined with public transportation infrastructure. Because of Rome's indifferent immigration policies, many migrants have been forced to make a living in the residual spaces between these overpasses.

Recent renovations around Tiburtina have made the area's spatial configuration increasingly difficult to inhabit and use. In response to these conditions and to unwelcoming immigration policies, the project proposes a new public space organized around a monument, providing both local residents and migrants with a new communal living area and temporary shelter.
