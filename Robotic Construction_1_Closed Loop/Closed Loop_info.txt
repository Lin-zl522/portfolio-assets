名为“Closed Loop”的项目，旨在利用生物塑料通过机器人3D 打印的方式打造一款供多人使用的座椅，并将其安装在大学校园的主要人行路线上，为行人提供休憩空间。作为一种公共家具，这款座椅不仅用于检验制造与可扩展性问题，也通过探索再生塑料在建筑构件中作为结构材料的潜力，为后续研究提供了重要参考。
本研究以再生PLA（聚乳酸）为主要材料——这是一种生物基聚合物，其拉伸模量可达3500 MPa，拉伸强度可达45 MPa。研究采用中型六轴ABB 机器人，配备Massive Dimension 螺杆泵式挤出机。相比传统的笛卡尔式3D 打印机，此种六轴系统能够实现更大幅度的打印角度和更宽裕的成型体积，并在打印过程中保持喷头垂直于工作面，从而在大规模增材制造中提升几何精度和效率。

[en]
Closed Loop uses robotically 3D-printed bioplastic to create a multi-person seat installed along a major pedestrian route on a university campus. As a piece of public furniture, the seat serves not only to test questions of fabrication and scalability, but also to investigate the structural potential of recycled plastic in architectural components, providing a foundation for further research.

The study uses recycled PLA (polylactic acid), a bio-based polymer with a tensile modulus of up to 3,500 MPa and a tensile strength of up to 45 MPa. Fabrication is carried out with a medium-scale six-axis ABB robot equipped with a Massive Dimension screw-pump extruder. Compared with a conventional Cartesian 3D printer, the six-axis system enables a wider range of printing angles and a larger build volume while keeping the nozzle perpendicular to the work surface, improving geometric precision and efficiency in large-scale additive manufacturing.
